Do I need riding experience to take lessons?

No. Lessons are available for complete beginners through advanced riders. Tell us about the rider’s experience so we can discuss the most suitable starting point.

Do I need my own horse?

No. NietBegin has well-trained, gentle lesson horses. Horse availability is discussed when you enquire.

How long is a riding lesson?

Lessons are approximately 30–40 minutes.

How much do lessons cost?

One lesson per week is R120 per week. Two lessons per week are R200 per week.

Are riding helmets provided?

Yes, helmets can be provided when needed.

How large are lesson groups?

Classes are kept small, with a maximum of four riders.

Why are lessons not booked on Mondays?

The upcoming week’s detailed lesson plan is prepared and emailed every Monday. Lessons are not scheduled that day.

How do I enquire about stabling?

Use the contact form and choose Stabling. Tell us about your horse, current routine and what you need from a yard. We will discuss availability before arranging a visit.

Are any horses currently for sale?

The website shows current availability and keeps sold horses in a previously placed archive. If no horse is listed as available, you can still tell us what you are looking for.

Can I visit the yard?

Yes, but all visits are by appointment. The yard is near Heidelberg, Gauteng; precise directions are shared after a visit is arranged.

Who should I contact?

General enquiries can be sent to info@nietbegin.co.za, by WhatsApp to 082 782 8761, or through the website contact form.
