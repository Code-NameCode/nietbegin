How enquiry information is handled.

A plain-language explanation of the personal information collected through this website and why it is needed.

  2. Privacy notice

Effective date: 29 August 2026

NietBegin Stables respects your privacy. This notice applies to information submitted through the website enquiry form or provided when you contact us directly.

Information we collect

The enquiry form may collect your name, email address, mobile number, enquiry type, the horse you are interested in, and the message you choose to send.

Why we use it

We use this information only to understand and respond to your enquiry, arrange a visit, or discuss a requested service. The website does not use enquiry details for a newsletter or unrelated marketing.

Email delivery

Form submissions are sent through our own secure form service, which delivers the enquiry to info@nietbegin.co.za. The service processes the details only to deliver your message, and its handling of information is governed by its own service and privacy terms.

Children’s information

If you are enquiring about lessons for a child, please provide a parent or guardian’s contact details. Do not include unnecessary sensitive information about a child in the message.

How long information is kept

Enquiry information is kept only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, provide the requested service and maintain appropriate business records.

Sharing and security

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by the communication services needed to deliver and respond to an enquiry. Reasonable steps are taken to limit access and protect the information under our control.

Your choices

You may ask what enquiry information we hold about you, request a correction, or ask that information no longer needed be deleted. Contact info@nietbegin.co.za or call 082 782 8761.

Questions or concerns

Contact us first so we can address your concern. You may also learn more about South Africa’s privacy framework from the Information Regulator.
