A lesson that meets you where you are

Riding is only part of what horses teach us.

With more than 10 years of experience, head instructor Stephanie Soutar teaches complete beginners through to experienced riders, always beginning with the horse in front of them.

Lessons include confidence, safety, horse care, responsibility and the quiet work of building a strong partnership.
