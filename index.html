Small yard, real attention

A stable built around the horse.

NietBegin is tucked away on a dairy farm just outside Heidelberg. It is a home-like yard where horses are treated as individuals and riders have space to grow.

Riders come here to spend meaningful time with horses: learning how they think, how to care for them and how trust grows through quiet, patient work.
